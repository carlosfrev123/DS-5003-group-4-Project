                     In aggregate, the AHA number of admissions and births (column M) is often higher than SID discharges (column C) for various reasons: (1) AHA data are based on a fiscal year counts of admissions, while the SID are calendar year 
                   counts of discharges, (2) by summing the AHA counts for admissions and births there may be some double counting of newborns treated in the NICU, and (3) AHA admission counts tend to include long-term care and swing bed 
                   admissions, which may not be included in the SID counts, depending on the state.
